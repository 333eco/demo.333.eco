When the phone's software was changed

This phone can't hold B-PoH keys

Its proof says it isn't running its maker's own software, so the keys in its chip can't be shown to be its own. Your passkey still works everywhere it did.

When the phone has no screen lock

Turn on a screen lock first

These keys unlock only with a PIN, or a finger set up on this phone. Set one in the phone's settings, then add the phone again.

When your account holds all the phones it can

Remove a phone first

Your account holds as many phone keys as it can. Remove one you no longer use, in Your keys, then add this one.
