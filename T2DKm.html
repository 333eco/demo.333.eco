Draft of 9 November 2026.

What this service is

This service keeps a private record of thanks given on HeartBank® apps and timestamps a fingerprint of each to a public ledger. You can open and download your own record.

HeartBank® is a planetary data bank of gratitude. It is not a chartered bank or financial institution; it does not provide banking services, hold deposits, or extend credit. Nothing is paid here: this service keeps a record of thanks and moves no money. Financial services are not provided by HeartBank.

Kiitos and Kiitti

Kiitos is a thank between two people. Kiitti is care for a thing, a plant or an animal.

  • They have no cash value. They cannot be bought, sold, traded, transferred, redeemed or exchanged for money, goods or time.
  • They are a record, not property. You have no ownership claim on a balance or a color.
