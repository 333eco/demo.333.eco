A private record of your thanks.

When you thank someone on a HeartBank® app, this service keeps an encrypted note that it happened. We timestamp a fingerprint of that note to a public ledger, so it can't be changed later without showing. The note itself stays private and yours: you can open it here and download it. What you wrote, and any amount or time, stays in the app you used. Nothing is paid here.
