Thank with HeartBank®

Record a thank on the gratitude ledger. Your app never writes a thank alone: the person signs it with their own passkey.

1 Prepare
Your server asks the ledger. It answers with a ticket.
2 The person presses
They open sign.333.eco, which shows them what they are signing, and press their passkey.
3 Commit
Your server sends the press back. The ledger writes the thank and gives you a receipt.

Outside apps: from season 2

Apps outside HeartBank® write from 7 January 2027, after review.

Kiitos, and Kiitti as thanks to all of life

An outside app writes Kiitos, and Kiitti only as a person's thanks to all of life. Care for a plant or an animal is witnessed, never claimed by an app.

A refusal says nothing

When the ledger declines a thank, your app is told only that it wasn't written. No reason and no count: it protects the people involved. It is not a bug.
