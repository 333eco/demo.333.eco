What signal means

B-Signal℠ is the AI markers found in the file, from 0 to 1. A higher number means more markers were found. It never measures how human a work is, and a low number doesn't prove a person made it.

One rule for your app

Don't show B-Signal as a grade of someone's work. Show what the file carries instead: the record, its label and its limit. Our terms require it.
